Three ideas worth testing

The gifting calendar

Ramadan, Eid and the winter season carry much of the region’s fragrance gifting. A launch tracker built around them would time sampling, gift sets and retail theatre to the weeks that matter.

Layering as a language

Fragrance layering is part of regional scent culture. A guided layering moment in store and on social could connect a Puig brand to a habit shoppers already have, instead of asking them to learn a new one.

The South Asian shopper

Dubai’s South Asian community is large and often overlooked in luxury beauty. I speak Telugu and Hindi, and Puig’s portfolio includes Kama Ayurveda, so I would test content and in-store storytelling for this audience.
